Most Dutch research universities charge €100 to applicants whose previous degree is from outside the Netherlands, and they keep it if you are turned down. But you can apply to some good Dutch universities without paying anything, one refunds the fee when you enrol, and how far €100 goes differs a lot from one university to the next. Here is each one, for applications made from autumn 2026.

Universities of applied sciences (hogescholen) teach practice-based bachelor's degrees and some master's, and they cost non-EU students far less than research universities. Their non-EU fees and the cheapest options are in our Netherlands guide.

The universities will not assess your file until the fee has arrived, so pay it as soon as you submit.

University of Groningen: who gets the fee waived

Groningen says it waives the application fee only in very specific cases:

  • students with refugee status in the Netherlands;
  • applicants to an Erasmus Mundus programme, although the programme itself may decide to charge a fee;
  • Double Degree applicants whose previous university has already assessed their diploma;
  • applicants with a Dutch secondary education diploma or a Dutch bachelor's degree;
  • applicants with a VWO diploma from Aruba, Bonaire, Curaçao, Sint Maarten, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Suriname.

Everyone else pays once per academic year, however many Groningen programmes they apply to, and the fee is not refunded. Applicants who qualify for an exemption are told by email how it works.

How to spend as little as possible

  • Start with the free ones. Wageningen, most Twente master's and the universities of applied sciences above cost nothing to apply to.
  • Pick universities where one fee covers several programmes. At VU Amsterdam one payment covers four programmes, at Utrecht it lasts three years, and at Groningen, Tilburg and Radboud it covers the whole academic year.
  • Count Maastricht as free if you will go. Its fee comes back once you start your studies there.
  • Watch the per-programme fees. Erasmus School of Economics and TU Eindhoven charge again for every application.

If the fee is a worry because the whole degree is, look at the scholarships that pay Dutch tuition: TU Delft's Van Effen, Maastricht's NL-High Potential and Leiden's Excellence Scholarship.
