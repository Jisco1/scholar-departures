The biggest mistake applicants make is starting too late. Scholarship deadlines fall long before university start dates. For study starting in September 2027, Chevening and Knight-Hennessy closed on 6 October 2026, ETH's ESOP runs from 1 to 30 November 2026, and Gates Cambridge closes on 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course: nine to eleven months before the first day of class.

This plan uses September 2027 entry as its example, and each heading counts the months back from September 2027. If your intake is different, move every step by the same number of months. Each scholarship page with a confirmed deadline also has a working timeline that counts back from that date; open any route from the routes list to see it.

June to September 2026 (15 to 12 months before): shortlist and prepare

  • Decide your level (bachelor's, master's, PhD) and two or three fields you could apply in.
  • Filter the scholarship board by your nationality, study level and field; eligibility rules are the fastest way to shorten a list.
  • Aim for a balanced list of 6–10 routes: a few highly competitive full scholarships, several solid options, and at least one affordable fallback such as a tuition-free or low-tuition university.
  • Note every deadline, including hidden ones: school nominations, fee-waiver requests, preliminary document checks such as Germany's uni-assist, and national nominating agencies.
  • Book any English or other tests you need, and allow time for a retake.
  • Ask your referees: lecturers, employers or teachers who know your work. Our guide to recommendation letters explains what to give them. For a PhD, start contacting potential supervisors with a short, specific email (see our supervisor guide).
  • Order transcripts, degree certificates and certified translations. Some take weeks; apostilles and legalisation can take longer. Our document checklist covers what to request.
  • Write a full first draft of your motivation letter or personal statement, and draft your CV in the format each route asks for (some want a Europass CV, some a one-page résumé, some a three-page academic CV). See how to write a motivation letter that wins funding.

October to December 2026 (11 to 9 months before): the early deadlines

These are the busiest months of the cycle. For September 2027 entry:

  • Chevening and Knight-Hennessy closed on 6 October 2026, and the Pearson Scholarship's school nominations on 9 October.
  • Commonwealth Master's Scholarships close on 20 October 2026 on CSC Central; your national nominating agency may close earlier.
  • US colleges: early rounds close between 1 and 15 November 2026 and regular rounds in the first days of January 2027, with financial aid forms such as the CSS Profile alongside. Each college's page here lists its rounds.
  • UBC International Scholars closes on 15 November 2026, ETH's ESOP runs from 1 to 30 November, TU Delft's Van Effen scholarship and the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship close on 1 December, and Gates Cambridge on 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course.
  • Revise each motivation letter for the specific route. Reusing one generic letter is the second most common reason for rejection, after missing deadlines.

January to March 2027 (8 to 6 months before): the second wave

  • KAUST's second round closes on 3 January 2027 (KAUST calls the date tentative), the University of Helsinki's international master's round runs from 5 to 19 January, Maastricht's NL-High Potential scholarship closes on 1 February, Ghent's doctoral scholarships on 9 February, and RWTH Aachen's non-EU master's applications for winter 2027/28 on 1 March.
  • Many Erasmus Mundus programmes and university admissions close between January and March. Stipendium Hungaricum and the Romanian government scholarship closed in that period in 2026; their 2027 calls, like Türkiye Scholarships', are not announced yet, so check their pages before you plan around them.
  • Prepare for interviews: practise explaining your plan in two minutes, and why this route and not another. Chevening, for example, interviews shortlisted candidates in March and April 2027.

April to June 2027 (5 to 3 months before): decisions and visas

  • Compare offers using the full yearly cost (tuition, fees, rent, insurance, visa, travel), not just the headline award.
  • Accept in writing by the deadline and decline the offers you will not take, so the place goes to someone else.
  • Start the visa process as soon as you accept. Appointment waiting times at some embassies run to months.

July and August 2027 (2 to 1 months before): arrival

  • Proof of funds, blocked account or bond, health insurance, and accommodation.
  • Keep digital and paper copies of every document you submitted.
Rule of thumb: submit every application at least a week before the deadline. Portals slow down on the last day, documents get rejected for formatting, and referees get ill. A week's margin turns a crisis into an inconvenience.
