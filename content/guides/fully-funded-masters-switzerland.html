Swiss universities charge international students modest tuition by world standards, but Zurich, Geneva and Lausanne are among the most expensive cities to live in. A fully funded master's here therefore means a scholarship that pays living costs, and only a handful do. These are the ones open to international students, with what each pays and when to apply for a start in autumn 2027.

Who each one is for

  • ETH Zurich ESOP is for outstanding students admitted to an ETH master's. You apply for it in the same November window as the master's itself, and ETH charges a CHF 150 application fee that it does not waive.
  • EPFL Excellence Fellowships go to a limited number of top applicants to EPFL's master's programmes in Lausanne. CHF 10,000 a semester more than covers EPFL's tuition, so the rest goes towards living costs.
  • The Geneva Graduate Institute awards aid on academic excellence and financial need, to master's students of any nationality. Admission decisions do not take your finances into account, but you can only ask for aid in the January round.
  • The University of Geneva's fellowships are for students starting a Master of Science in its Faculty of Science, in subjects such as physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics and computer science.
  • The University of Lausanne funds graduates of foreign universities with excellent results; the scholarship application also counts as the master's application.

What the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship does not cover

The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships are the best known Swiss awards, but they fund doctoral and postdoctoral research, at CHF 2,450 a month, not taught master's programmes. Apply for them if you are heading for a PhD in Switzerland; for a master's, look at the five above.

Where there is no general master's scholarship

Some large Swiss universities do not fund international master's students at all. The University of Zurich's Student Financial Aid Office says: "We can not provide support to international Master's students who do not hold Swiss Bachelor's degrees." Check a university's own funding page before counting on aid there.

How to plan your applications

  • Start with ETH. Its window closes on 30 November 2026, weeks before the others.
  • Then EPFL and Geneva. EPFL's fellowship needs your master's application validated by 15 December, and the Graduate Institute's aid round closes on 14 January 2027.
  • Apply to more than one. Each scholarship funds only a few dozen students at most, so one application is a long shot.
