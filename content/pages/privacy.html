Who we are

Information stored on your own device

The site uses your browser's local storage — not cookies — for features you choose to use:

  • Your shortlist. When you bookmark a route, the route's name is saved in your browser so your shortlist is there when you return. It never leaves your device, and you can remove it by un-bookmarking the route or clearing your browser's site data.
  • A premium licence key, only if we offer optional paid tools and you enter a key you bought. The key is stored in your browser and sent to our payment provider, Gumroad, to confirm it is valid. Gumroad's handling of that request is covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Calendar files you export are created in your browser and downloaded directly to your device; we do not receive them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical information your browser sends with any web request, such as your IP address, and may log it for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Advertising

When advertising is shown on this site, it is served by Google AdSense. Ads appear only in spaces labelled "Advertisements".

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy choices" link in the footer, where it appears.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to act on your report, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Links to other websites

Route pages link to universities, scholarship providers and governments. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

The site is intended for people researching university study and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not run accounts or collect personal data ourselves, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete; what is stored lives in your own browser, under your control. For data held by Google or GitHub, use the links above. If you have a question or request, email {{email}}.

Changes

If we change how the site handles information — for example by adding a newsletter — we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
