Reporting a correction

Please include:

  1. the name of the route (or a link to its page on this site),
  2. what is wrong — for example the deadline, eligibility or what the award covers,
  3. a link to the official page that shows the correct information.

We check every report against the official source before changing anything, and update the page's "Last verified" date when we do.

Suggesting a route

We welcome suggestions that meet our listing rules: free tuition for international students, a full scholarship covering tuition and living costs, full-need financial aid, or an application fee waiver. A link to the official page is essential.

What we can't help with

  • We can't assess your individual eligibility or chances, review your documents, or submit applications for you.
  • We are not connected to any university or scholarship provider, so we cannot contact them on your behalf. Questions about a specific programme should go to the contact listed on its official page.
Protect yourself from scams. {{site}} never charges fees, never asks for passwords, bank details or copies of your passport, and never contacts you first to offer a scholarship. Genuine scholarships do not ask you to pay to receive them. Read our guide to spotting scholarship scams.
