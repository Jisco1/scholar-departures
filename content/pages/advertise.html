The rules

  • Only a programme that already meets our listing rules can be sponsored. Paying does not get a programme listed.
  • The spot always carries the word "Sponsored".
  • We write your programme's page ourselves, from your official pages. Sponsoring does not change what it says, or its place in the main list and in search results.
  • We do not collect student details for you, and we do not sell visitor data. Students apply through your own process.
  • A spot runs for up to 31 days and comes down on its end date.
