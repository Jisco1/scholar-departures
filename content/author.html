Eric Arko founded {{site}} and edits its guides. He is a petroleum engineer from Ghana, and he started the site in September 2026 while preparing his own applications for funded master's places in Europe and Japan.

Background

  • Education. B.Sc. Petroleum Engineering with First Class Honours from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Kumasi, 2022. He led the group whose final-year thesis studied how burrowing organisms change the porosity and permeability of reservoir rock.
  • Work. Stocks and quality control officer at a fuel depot in Ghana's downstream petroleum industry.
  • Mentoring. He mentored first-year students at KNUST and has tutored secondary-school students in science and mathematics.

How the guides are written

Each guide is built from the official pages of the governments, universities and funders it covers. Research and first drafts are done with the help of AI tools; Eric reviews them and decides what {{site}} publishes. The editorial policy sets out where the facts come from and how they are kept current.
